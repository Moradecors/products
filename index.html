Our History

Preserving the Art of a Legacy.

Founded in 1972 in Attukal, Trivandrum, Kerala, MORA emerged at a turning point in Indian history. The traditional handicraft sector, once rich and vibrant, was declining due to colonial exploitation and post independence industrialization. In this environment, M.R. Krishnan, a national & state award winning artisan, established MORA not just as a business, but as a living tribute to India’s artistic and spiritual heritage. Rooted in a proud four generation legacy of rare sculptural craftsmanship, MORA represents the seamless blend of age old tradition and refined artistry. Our master artisans shape each creation by hand, preserving techniques passed down through generations to produce timeless, museum worthy masterpieces. MORA is dedicated to safeguarding and celebrating the rich heritage of Indian craftsmanship.
