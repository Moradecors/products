ABOUT MORA

Four Generations of Craftsmanship

Since 1972, MORA has preserved four generations of rare Indian craftsmanship, creating sculptural pieces where heritage meets refined luxury.

OUR LEGACY

The Attukal Legacy

Founded in Attukal by National Awardee M.R. Krishnan, MORA carries a legacy of exceptional craftsmanship, artistry, and cultural heritage.

MATERIAL

Pieces of Rare Character

Coconut, lemongrass, banana paper, silk, cotton, linen, jute, and polyester, thoughtfully chosen to create pieces of rare character and beauty.
